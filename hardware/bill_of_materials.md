# Bill of materials

Source: Bill of Engineering Measurement and Evaluation (BEME), thesis Chapter 3. Prices are as printed in the thesis (Naira). The BEME lists everything purchased, including tools and consumables, not only what appears in the final circuit.

**Purchased versus used (from code and builder statements):** the BEME lists 4 MG996R and 3 SG90; the code drives 3 MG996R and 2 SG90. It lists 1 Nano and 2 Uno; the final build used Unos for both subsystems after the Nano was lost (see docs/failures.md). The stepper motor, the 18650 cells, the BMS and the 7805 regulators do not appear in the code or in the circuit diagrams, and their use is NOT PROVIDED.
